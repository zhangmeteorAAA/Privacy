隐私政策

到岗 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 14 日

我们处理哪些信息

到岗不要求创建账号，不包含广告或分析 SDK，也不向应用开发者的服务器上传打卡记录、位置或设置。

当你选择定位打卡，或启用定时后台打卡时，应用会请求 iOS 定位权限并使用设备位置。应用也可能调用 Apple 的系统逆地理编码服务，将坐标转换为地点名称；该请求由 Apple 系统服务处理。你可以改用手动打卡。

本机保存

打卡记录、你设置的公司或家庭位置以及应用偏好保存在设备本机。应用开发者无法通过本应用读取这些数据。打卡记录可在应用设置中清除；删除应用会移除本机应用数据。设备备份由你使用的 Apple 服务管理。

权限与选择

你可以在 iOS「设置」中关闭定位或通知权限。关闭定位后，定位打卡和后台定位打卡将不可用；手动打卡仍可使用。你也可以随时关闭后台打卡。

第三方服务

应用使用 Apple 提供的 iOS 定位与逆地理编码服务。此类服务的数据处理适用 Apple 的隐私政策：apple.com/legal/privacy。

政策更新

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面和生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Clock In Calendar · Effective November 14, 2026

Information We Process

Clock In Calendar does not require an account, contain advertising or analytics SDKs, or upload check-in records, location, or settings to a server operated by the app developer.

When you choose a location-based check-in or enable scheduled background check-ins, the app requests iOS location permission and uses your device location. The app may also use Apple’s system reverse-geocoding service to turn coordinates into a place name. Apple’s system service processes that request. Manual check-in is also available.

Storage on Your Device

Check-in records, places you configure, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. The app developer cannot access this information through the app. You can clear check-in records in the app’s Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Device backups are managed by the Apple services you use.

Your Choices

You can turn off location or notification permissions in iOS Settings. Location-based and background check-ins will not work when location access is disabled; manual check-in remains available. You can also turn off background check-ins at any time.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple’s iOS location and reverse-geocoding services. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to data processed by those services: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Changes to This Policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
