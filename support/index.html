Clock In Calendar Support

Help for check-ins, location permissions, and settings.

Check-in options

You can check in manually, use your current location, or enable scheduled background check-ins. Location-based features require Location Services permission. Manual check-in remains available when location access is off.

Location or scheduled check-in is not working

  • Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and allow location access for Clock In Calendar.
  • For scheduled check-ins, allow the location access requested by iOS and keep scheduled check-ins enabled in the app.
  • Check that the configured place and distance are correct. You can always use manual check-in.

Records and data

Check-in records, saved places, and preferences are stored on your device. You can clear check-in records in the app’s Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data.

到岗支持

打卡、定位权限和设置帮助

打卡方式

你可以手动打卡、使用当前位置打卡，或开启定时后台打卡。定位功能需要允许 iOS 定位权限。关闭定位后仍可手动打卡。

定位或定时打卡无法使用

  • 打开 iOS「设置」→「隐私与安全性」→「定位服务」，允许「到岗」使用定位。
  • 定时打卡需要按 iOS 提示允许定位，并在 App 内保持定时打卡开启。
  • 检查已设置的地点和距离是否正确。你也可以使用手动打卡。

记录与数据

打卡记录、保存的地点和偏好设置保存在你的设备上。可在 App「设置」中清除打卡记录。删除 App 会移除本机 App 数据。
